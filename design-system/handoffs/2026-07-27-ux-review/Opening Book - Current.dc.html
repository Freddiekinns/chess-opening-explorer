Sicilian Defence:Najdorf Variation

advanced aggressive dynamic theoretical sharp
{{ boardDesktop }}
Practice
Start 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 d6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 a6
Overview

The Najdorf is the most storied of the Open Sicilians. With 5…a6 Black denies White's pieces the b5 square and prepares …e5 or …e6, keeping the centre flexible. It leads to the sharpest theory in chess — the English Attack, the Poisoned Pawn, the Fischer–Sozin — and rewards deep preparation and fearless play on both wings.

Common plans

Shared

Play revolves around the d5 square and the e4 pawn. The kings typically castle on opposite wings, and the game becomes a race — each side storming pawns toward the enemy king, tempo for tempo.

White

Launch a kingside pawn storm with f3, g4 and h4 after Be3 and Qd2 — the English Attack.

Contest the d5 square and prepare a Nd5 sacrifice to shatter Black's structure.

Black

Counterattack on the queenside with …b5, …Bb7 and pressure down the half-open c-file.

Time an …e5 or …d5 break to free the position when White over-extends on the kingside.
