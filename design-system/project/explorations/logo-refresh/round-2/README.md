# Logo refresh: round two

Round one's ribbon pawn was rejected because it reads as a person. It is also
too close to chess.com, whose logo is a lone pawn (Lichess has the knight). This
round starts from what the product is: a calm reference book of 12,377
positions, where orange is the bookmark ribbon.

- **B: Pawn on the page.** Round one's B redrawn with a Staunton pawn that grows
  out of the open book. Familiar and safe, but pawn-on-book is the category's
  stock image.
- **F: The board is the book** (recommended). An open book whose spread is a
  strip of board, with the chequer running across the spine and the orange
  ribbon in the gutter. Book, board and bookmark in one shape, with no pawn.
  `f-board-book-small` (one light page, one dark page) is its 16–24px cut.

`concepts.png` compares both with the current mark.
