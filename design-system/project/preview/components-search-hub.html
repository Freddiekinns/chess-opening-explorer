Your repertoire

The pre-typing state of every search surface — desktop's dropdown previously showed nothing at all until you typed. All three surfaces render this: the top-bar dropdown, the mobile overlay, and the Discover hero field. If a search surface gains a pre-typing state, it uses this hub; do not draw a second one.

This card owns the sections, not the rows. Rows come from components-search-row — the same row the results list draws, so typing changes which openings are listed and nothing else about them. The two used to diverge on ten counts including the type scale itself. Do not restyle a row here to suit the hub.

The panel needs its own padding. This dropdown shipped with none at all, so "Recent" sat one pixel below the top border and read as clipped, and every row touched the sides. The mobile overlay never showed the fault because its scrolling body supplies the inset — a reminder that a shared component can still be broken by exactly one of its containers.

Typing two characters replaces the sections with the ranked list in components-search-results. The two states are continuous rather than exclusive: the hub's sections give way, but Surprise me carries through as a footer, and repertoire membership carries through as a "Saved" badge on the matching rows. Nothing the hub offers should be reachable only before you type.
