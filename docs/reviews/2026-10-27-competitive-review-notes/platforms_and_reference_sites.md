# Platforms and free opening reference sites, and how AI search affects them (as of October 2026)

Method note: WebFetch is blocked (lichess.org would not even resolve), so
everything below comes from WebSearch result summaries. Snippets were not
checked against full pages, and the dates on older sources are given wherever
they are known. The WebSearch tool is US-only and does not give a real Google
ranking order, so the "who ranks" findings are only indicative.

## (a) Chess.com and Lichess opening features: what is free and what is paid

### Takeaway

Both platforms now bundle opening learning into the play loop. Chess.com's
approach is commercial: Chessable's MoveTrainer was moved in as "Courses" in
September 2026, Game Review checks your games against the courses you own, and
depth is metered by tier. Lichess gives all of its data away (masters, Lichess
games by rating band, and a per-player explorer), and it has static opening
pages with text descriptions. Neither offers one free page per opening that
brings together plans, style, videos, studies, per-band stats and practice; that
combination is what openingbook.xyz does.

**Lichess (all free)**

- The explorer has three tabs. Masters covers curated over-the-board games
  (about 2M at launch, c. 2016). The Lichess tab filters by time control and
  rating range. The Player tab shows any user's games and filters by colour,
  time control, period and rated/casual, so it is useful for scouting opponents.
  —
  [Lichess blog: Opening Explorer](https://lichess.org/@/lichess/blog/opening-explorer/Vs0xMTAA);
  [Personal Opening Explorer](https://lichess.org/blog/YXMPxxMAACEAy3g4/announcing-the-personal-opening-explorer);
  [lila-openingexplorer](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila-openingexplorer)
- Lichess counts how often each move was played. A forum comparison says
  Chess.com's explorer counts resulting positions, so transpositions are
  included. —
  [Chess.com forum](https://www.chess.com/forum/view/chess-openings/opening-explorer-doesnt-actually-show-the-popularity-of-moves-lichess-is-better)
- The lichess.org/opening index lists openings by family, with share of games,
  W/D/L, and links to the explorer. —
  [Lichess Opening](https://lichess.org/opening)
- Lichess aims to "document all named openings with a text description". It
  separates "keystone" pages (Ruy Lopez, Sicilian) with longer text from smaller
  pages, contributors are recruited by message, and there is no built-in way to
  suggest edits (guide c. 2022). —
  [Writing Lichess Opening Pages](https://lichess.org/page/opening-content)
- Wikibooks Chess Opening Theory snippets have appeared next to the analysis
  board since October 2021, text only, with limited transposition support. A
  lila issue reports that the Openings page truncates the Wikibooks intro at
  1,200 characters, cutting sentences mid-way. —
  [Wikibooks: How to navigate](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Chess_Opening_Theory/How_to_navigate_this_wikibook);
  [lila issue #17957](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/issues/17957)
- Opening names come from the open dataset lichess-org/chess-openings, which is
  also on Kaggle and Hugging Face. —
  [GitHub chess-openings](https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings)
- Lichess also offers puzzles by opening. —
  [Lichess training/openings](https://lichess.org/training/openings)
- 2026 changes are small. In March (mobile), FEN and PGN sharing from the
  explorer. In June (mobile), the opening name in the broadcast and study
  explorer. In August, a sticky header and an icon per database. A mid-2026 blog
  post says many openings were "added since the beginning of the year". —
  [Lichess changelog](https://lichess.org/changelog);
  [March 2026 changelog thread](https://lichess.org/forum/lichess-feedback/lichess-changelog-march-2026);
  [More Than a Half-Year Update 2026](https://lichess.org/@/lichess/blog/more-than-a-half-year-update-2026/Bu77Rba1)
- LiChess Tools (Siderite) "Explorer Practice" has the computer reply using
  explorer frequencies from Masters, Lichess (with rating range) or Player data,
  and gives an engine smiley when the line leaves the database. The latest
  version is v2.4.201 (27 May 2026) with more than 10K users, figures from a
  mirror listing. —
  [Lichess forum](https://lichess.org/forum/lichess-feedback/practice-with-opening-explorer-in-lichess-tools);
  [Siderite blog](https://siderite.dev/blog/how-to-learn-and-test-any-opening-in-hours-with-li/);
  [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/langlhlcknngldkeliapahbhbcmlcbcj)
- Semrush (modelled) puts lichess.org organic traffic at 7.93M (+9%) in
  August 2026. —
  [Semrush lichess.org](https://www.semrush.com/website/lichess.org/overview/)

### Inferences

- Chess.com's 2026 roadmap builds a loop from paid course to Game Review to
  course. That is retention for paying users, not free reference content, so the
  free per-opening explainer niche is still open there.
- Lichess pages are the nearest free equivalent: text, stats and explorer.
  However, its descriptions depend on volunteers and are uneven, and the page as
  described has no curated videos, style tags or "plans" sections. Rating-band
  stats are in the explorer, not on the page.
- Explorer Practice in LiChess Tools shows demand for a "practise against real
  move frequencies" mode. Openingbook.xyz's practice mode competes with it
  directly, and a free extension already serves the power-user segment.

### Gaps

- No current official figure for Chess.com's free explorer depth. The "4 moves"
  claim comes from an undated, possibly stale support article.
- No details on the October 2026 "revamp of openings in Game Review", and
  nothing found on whether Coach covers openings beyond naming them.
- Exact current per-page contents of lichess.org/opening/...
  (popularity-over-time chart, top games) could not be confirmed because
  fetching was blocked.

## (b) Free opening-reference sites competing for the same search traffic

### Takeaway

The field is split between database explorers (365chess, chessgames.com,
ChessBase online), which show many games but little prose, and prose sites
(Wikipedia, Wikibooks, The Chess World, retailer and affiliate blogs, chess.com
articles), which have no live data. For head terms, results go to Wikipedia,
chess.com, Lichess video pages and course sellers. No competitor found combines
prose, per-band stats, videos and practice on one free page.

### Cited Findings

- 365chess: an online database of more than 4.4M games, updated weekly. Its free
  explorer shows W/D/L per continuation, and some forum users complain about
  low-quality games. In June 2026 Google organic was 47.5% of its traffic and
  ChatGPT sent only 772 visits (Semrush estimate). It also publishes SEO
  articles such as "Best Chess Openings for Beginners" and sells courses. —
  [365chess](https://www.365chess.com/);
  [365chess explorer](https://www.365chess.com/opening.php);
  [Semrush 365chess](https://www.semrush.com/website/365chess.com/overview/);
  [365chess beginners article](https://365chess.com/view/best-chess-openings-for-beginners/)
- chessgames.com: a free explorer. An old snapshot (© 2003–2019) showed about
  2.17M games with W/D/L by move, and premium features exist. —
  [chessgames explorer](https://www.chessgames.com/perl/explorer);
  [chessgames premium help](https://chessgames.com/premiumhelp.html)
- ChessBase online database: forum users call it "massive and regularly
  updated". —
  [Lichess forum: which explorers](https://lichess.org/forum/general-chess-discussion/what-online-chess-opening-explorers-you-use);

### Inferences

- Many commercial results for variation queries are course storefronts (The
  Chess World, 365chess, Wholesale Chess, Chessable). A free, substantive
  explainer page can compete on intent ("guide", "plans", "ideas").
- The long tail (around 12k named variations) is thinly served: Wikibooks is
  patchy, Lichess text is uneven, and spam fills the gaps. That is where
  openingbook.xyz's page count matters most.
- Head terms ("sicilian najdorf") are owned by Wikipedia and the Knowledge
  Graph, so they will be the most exposed to AI Overviews.

### Gaps

- chessopenings.com and Chess Tempo's database returned no usable results, so
  their page contents are unknown.
- No real SERP or rank-tracker data. A tool such as Ahrefs or Semrush with a
  UK/US locale would be needed to say who ranks first.
- No traffic estimates were found for Wikibooks chess pages, The Chess World or
  chessgames.com.

## (c) Evidence on how Google AI Overviews and AI search affect reference sites

### Takeaway

The evidence consistently shows fewer clicks when an AI Overview or AI Mode
appears. The size depends on method: about 35–58% lower CTR for the top result
(Ahrefs, observational), clicks almost halved from 15% to 8% (Pew), and −39.8%
organic clicks in a randomised study. AI referrals are growing fast but remain a
small share of traffic (under 1% for most sites). No study isolates chess, and
chess sites' traffic estimates show no clear AI-driven collapse yet.

### Cited Findings

- Pew (22 July 2025; 900 US adults' March 2025 browsing): users clicked a
  traditional result on 8% of visits with an AI summary versus 15% without one.
  Only 1% of visits clicked a link cited in the AI Overview, sessions ended
  without a click 26% of the time versus 16%, and 58% of users saw at least one
  AI Overview that month. —
  [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)
- Ahrefs: the original 2025 study found −34.5% CTR at position 1 when an AI
  Overview was present. The update (published around February 2026, December
  2025 data, 300k keywords) found −58% at position 1, −50.8% at position 2,
  −46.4% at position 3 and about −19.4% at position 10. These are correlational,
  and Google disputes them. —
  [Ahrefs update](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/?pp=iaqb);
  [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-google-ai-overviews-click-through-rates-58-study/)
- Seer Interactive (September 2025; 3,119 informational terms, 42 organisations,
  25.1M organic impressions): organic CTR with an AI Overview fell from 1.76%
  (June 2024) to 0.61%. Without an AI Overview it was 1.62%, still down 41% year
  on year, which suggests broader shifts to ChatGPT and other platforms. Brands
  cited in the AI Overview got 35% more organic clicks than uncited ones. Seer's
  own baseline figures are slightly inconsistent (2.72 vs 2.74). —
  [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update);
  [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-drive-drop-organic-paid-ctr-464212)
- Similarweb (news queries only): zero-click news searches rose from 56%
  (May 2024) to 69% (May 2025). Organic visits to news sites fell from over 2.3B
  to under 1.7B. ChatGPT referrals to news sites grew from under 1M
  (January–May 2024) to over 25M (January–May 2025), not enough to offset the
  loss. —
  [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/similarweb-google-zero-click-search-growth-39706.html?frame=0);
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/02/chatgpt-referrals-to-news-sites-are-growing-but-not-enough-to-offset-search-declines)
- Randomised evidence, 2026:
  - UPenn/Northeastern field experiment (arXiv 18 August 2026; 1,100 US users):
    forced AI Mode cut external click-through by 18.8 percentage points, and
    hiding AI Overviews raised it by 8.8 points. Wikipedia clicks fell 9.9
    points, news 12.5 and Reddit 21.2. AI Mode users switched to other engines
    11.2 points more often and rated trust and satisfaction lower. Only about
    51% of AI Overviews were successfully hidden, which was adjusted for
    statistically.
  - A separate randomised study (1,065 desktop Chrome users) found −39.8%
    organic clicks and +34.5% zero-click searches when an AI Overview showed,
    with no change in satisfaction.
  - In May 2026 Google added "Further Exploration" links to AI Overviews. —
    [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-mode-cuts-clicks-satisfaction-study-490494);
    [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/geo/ai-mode-field-experiment-clicks-drop-18-8-points/);
    [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-overviews-cut-publisher-clicks-39-8-in-first-randomized-study/);
    [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-ai-overviews-publisher-links-search-traffic)
- Wikipedia, the closest analogue to a free reference site: human pageviews fell
  about 8% year on year (reported October 2025; windows given as March–August or
  May–August 2025, after reclassifying bot traffic). Wikimedia attributes the
  fall to search engines answering directly and to social video. —
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/wikipedia-says-traffic-is-falling-due-to-ai-search-summaries-and-social-video);
  [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/344845/wikipedia-traffic-drops-ai-answers-eat-free-encyclopedia)
- AI referrals in 2026:
  - Statcounter: AI chatbots were 0.25% of worldwide referrals in August 2026,
    peaking at 0.43% in April and May 2026. —
    [upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/ai-traffic-share-report-2026/)
  - SE Ranking (101,574 sites): ChatGPT's share of AI referrals was about 76% in
    2026, down from about 80%, ahead of Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Claude.
    ChatGPT referrals hit an all-time high in May 2026, and 60% of AI-referred
    visits land on homepages versus 17% from organic search. —
    [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/chatgpt-referral-traffic-may-2026/)
  - Similarweb: ChatGPT's share of generative-AI site visits fell from about 76%
    (June 2025) to about 53% (May 2026). —
    [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)
- Chess-specific (modelled Semrush or Ahrefs figures only): chess.com received
  about 274K visits from ChatGPT and 35K from Gemini. 365chess got 772 ChatGPT
  visits in June 2026, against Google organic at 47.5% of its traffic. Semrush
  shows lichess.org organic at +9% (August 2026). No source attributes a
  chess-site decline to AI Overviews. —
  [Semrush chess.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/chess.com/overview/);
  [Semrush 365chess](https://www.semrush.com/website/365chess.com/overview/);
  [Semrush lichess](https://www.semrush.com/website/lichess.org/overview/)

### Inferences

- The defensible range for planning is that an AI Overview roughly halves clicks
  to the top informational result, and costs less lower down the page. Being
  cited in the overview recovers part of that loss (Seer: +35%).
- Definitional opening queries ("what is the Najdorf", move orders, history) are
  prime territory for AI Overviews. Interactive, data-driven elements (live
  per-band stats, practice, curated videos) are harder for a summary to replace,
  and they are the reasons a user would still click through.
- AI referrals favour homepages and brands, so a long-tail reference site gets
  little from ChatGPT directly. Its main AI exposure is to Google's own
  overviews.

### Gaps

- No chess-specific study of AI Overview prevalence on opening queries. Neither
  the share of "caro kann advance" style queries that trigger an AI Overview nor
  the sources it cites was measured.
- Ahrefs' original source for its 2026 ChatGPT-share panel was not verified, and
  the AI-referral figures conflict between studies because their methods differ.
- No UK-specific data. All the CTR studies are US-based.
